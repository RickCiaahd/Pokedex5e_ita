## Full description

```text
Trainer Atlas 5e is an unofficial companion for managing 5e tabletop campaigns based on Trainers and pocket creatures.

The app brings together the main tools for players and Game Masters:

• create and manage Trainer profiles;
• organize your team, storage, Pokédex and inventory;
• browse Pokémon, moves, abilities, items and progression;
• edit statistics, experience, loyalty, conditions and learned moves;
• prepare encounters, NPC Trainers and Game Master support tools;
• export and import profile backups;
• switch between English and Italian.

The app works offline: the data and images required for normal use are included in the package. It does not require an online account and the current version does not include advertising or analytics systems.

Game data is stored locally on the device. Users can export or share files through apps selected in the operating system.

Trainer Atlas 5e is an amateur, unofficial project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by the owners of any third-party trademarks or content referenced or represented. Trademarks, characters, images and other content belong to their respective owners.
```
